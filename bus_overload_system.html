Threshold Logic
LevelConditionOccupancyResponseIndicator
NORMALpax < 82% of capacity< 82%No action needed🟢 Green bar
WARNING82% ≤ pax ≤ 100%82–100%Early dispatcher alert🟡 Amber bar
OVERLOADpax > 100% capacity> 100%Red alert + extra bus🔴 Red blinking
WHY 82% FOR WARNING? — Buses fill rapidly at stops. When a bus is already 82% full and 10+ passengers board at the next stop, it immediately overloads. The 82% threshold gives dispatchers 1–2 stops of reaction time to deploy an extra bus before the overload actually occurs.

Key Findings & Recommendations
🎯
97.76% ACCURACY
Random Forest correctly classified 2,874 out of 2,940 test trips. Production-ready for real dispatch integration.
⏰
PEAK HOUR = 41%
Rush hour is the single biggest predictor. Overload rate jumps from 2% off-peak to 77%+ during 7–9 AM and 5–8 PM.
🚌
LOCAL BUSES WORST
Local buses overload most (33%+) due to more stops, longer dwell time, and higher boarding rates per stop.
📏
STOP DISTANCES
400m–1.2km between stops in Bengaluru. Closer stops mean more boarding events and higher cumulative overload risk.
🔁
LAG FEATURE: 13.9%
Previous trip occupancy is 3rd most important feature — packed buses tend to stay packed across consecutive trips.
🔮
REAL DEPLOYMENT
Replace simulator with real GPS + IoT passenger counters. Model serves via REST API, pushes alerts to dispatcher app.
